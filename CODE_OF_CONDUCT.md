# Code of Conduct

This project is a small template maintained by a single owner. Contributors and
maintainers are expected to keep participation professional, technical, and
welcoming. This document is written in the spirit of the Contributor Covenant 2.1.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful of differing viewpoints, experience levels, and backgrounds.
- Give and accept constructive, specific feedback about the work.
- Assume good faith and ask questions before assigning motives.
- Keep discussion focused on the project and the outcome it serves.
- Respect the owner's decisions on scope, non-goals, and acceptance.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal or political attacks.
- Discriminatory comments or imagery.
- Sexualized language or unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Any conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, including issues, pull requests,
reviews, and any communication made on behalf of the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately through GitHub: open the repository
**Security** tab and choose **Report a vulnerability**, or contact the maintainer
through GitHub directly. Do not use a public issue. Reports are reviewed at the
maintainer's discretion; consequences range from a private warning to a temporary
or permanent ban from the project. No email address is used for reports.
