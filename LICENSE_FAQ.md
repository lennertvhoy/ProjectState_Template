# License FAQ

This is a plain-language guide to the repository license. The license text in
`LICENSE` is authoritative.

## Can I use this for personal projects?

Yes. You may use, copy, modify, and distribute the Software for personal
projects.

## Can I use this for commercial or client projects?

Yes. You may use the Software for commercial work, client work, internal
business tooling, paid products, and profit-making projects.

## Can I sell software or services that use this template?

Yes. You may sell software, templates, implementation services, consulting
services, or managed projects that use the Software, as long as you are not
selling teaching, training, courses, workshops, tutorials, curricula, coaching,
or educational services based on the Software or the ProjectState workflow.

## Can I teach this workflow?

No, not without prior written permission from the copyright holder. Teaching,
training, courses, workshops, tutorials, curricula, coaching, and educational
products or services based on the Software or ProjectState workflow are reserved
rights.

## Can I explain it to my team or clients?

Yes, when the explanation is incidental to permitted use. You may explain how
to install, configure, operate, maintain, support, or contribute to a project
that uses the Software.

## Can I publish a course or tutorial about it if it is free?

No. The teaching restriction applies whether the teaching activity is free or
paid, public or private, online or offline.

## Can I fork or modify it?

Yes. You may fork, modify, and distribute the Software, provided you keep the
license notice and respect the reserved teaching rights.

## Is this legal advice?

No. This FAQ is a practical explanation of the intended license policy. Ask a
qualified lawyer to review the final license before relying on it commercially.
